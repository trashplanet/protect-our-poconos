GLOSSARY

Terms You'll See

Following a zoning fight for the first time? These terms come up a lot.

Conditional use
A land use allowed only if specific zoning standards are met and approval is granted through the required public process.
Land-development approval
The detailed engineering and site-plan review generally required before construction can begin.
Hyperscale data center
A very large computing facility designed to support major cloud, AI, and digital workloads.
MW / megawatt
A measure of electrical power. Large data centers are often described by their proposed MW load.
Transmission line
High-voltage infrastructure that carries electricity between substations and major loads.
Party status
Formal participation status in certain land-use proceedings, providing additional procedural rights and responsibilities.
Overlay district
A zoning layer that adds or changes rules for specific properties or areas.
Curative amendment
A process used to correct or challenge a zoning ordinance that may be legally defective or exclusionary.

Practical Energy & Backup Power Resources

Concerns about electricity demand often raise practical questions closer to home: What am I paying for electricity? Where am I wasting energy? What should I keep powered during an outage? These resources help you understand your energy use, cut unnecessary consumption, compare electricity options, and prepare for outages safely.

These are general household resources. Their inclusion does not imply that any particular data-center project will cause higher electric bills or power outages.
