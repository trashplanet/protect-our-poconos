WHAT WE KNOW — AND WHAT WE DON'T

Separating Facts from Unknowns

Informed, evidence-based discussion is critical for a strong movement. Here's a look at what is established, what's still being studied, and what isn't yet backed by evidence.

Known Impacts
  • Land-use change
  • High electricity demand
  • Operational noise and backup-generator emissions
  • Permanent staffing that is relatively small compared with project scale
? Uncertain / Site-Specific
  • On-site water demand and groundwater impacts
  • Long-term community health effects
  • Cumulative impacts of multiple projects
  • Effects on future electricity costs
Not Currently Supported by Public Evidence
  • A data center has been formally proposed at Eagle Village
  • The Shawnee–Walker transmission project was built specifically for data centers
  • Every data center uses millions of gallons of water per day

Sources: DOE: electricity demand · SRBC: water use · FirstEnergy: transmission purpose · Smithfield: proposal and staffing
